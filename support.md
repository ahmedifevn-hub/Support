## Subscriptions & billing

- **Manage or cancel:** open the iPhone **Settings** app → tap your name → **Subscriptions** → **UARC** → cancel or change your plan. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID.
- **Restore purchases:** in UARC, open the paywall and tap **Restore Purchases** to bring back an active subscription on a new device.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Your account & data

- **Delete your account:** in UARC, go to **Settings → Delete account** and confirm. This permanently removes your profile, food logs, meal photos, workouts, weights and coach conversations. Can’t get into the app? Email us from your account address and we’ll delete it for you.
- **Deleting your account doesn’t cancel your subscription.** Cancel it in your Apple ID subscriptions too, or you’ll keep being charged.
- **Get a copy of your data:** email us and we’ll send it to you.
- **Privacy:** see our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy.html' | relative_url }}).
- **Terms:** see our [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms.html' | relative_url }}).

## Common questions

- **Switch language:** UARC is Arabic and English — change it in the app’s Settings.
- **Is the coach medical advice?** No. For pain, injury, medication or disordered eating, please see a qualified professional.
- **Something looks wrong?** Email us with your device model and a screenshot and we’ll sort it out.
