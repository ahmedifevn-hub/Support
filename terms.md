## 6. AI-generated content, and what "estimate" means

Much of what UARC shows you is generated by AI or calculated by formula. **It is
an estimate, and estimates are sometimes wrong.**

- **Food and macro numbers are estimates.** When you search a food, scan a
  barcode, or photograph a meal, the calorie and macro figures come from public
  food databases or from an AI model reading your photo. They will not match the
  actual food on your plate exactly. Weighing your food is more accurate than
  photographing it.
- **The coach can be wrong.** The AI coach reads your logged data before it
  replies, which makes it specific — but it is a language model, and it can
  still be mistaken, incomplete, or out of date. Don't treat it as fact-checked.
- **Weight and progress projections are estimates.** Where the app shows a
  projected or estimated weight change, it marks it as an estimate (with a `~`
  or a dashed line). Those numbers are derived from what you logged, not
  measured, and the app says so on screen.
- **We don't promise accuracy or results.** We don't guarantee any particular
  weight, strength, body-composition, or health outcome from using UARC.

Always use your own judgement, and check anything that matters.
